# Icon Assets

App icons in `public/icons/apps` are self-hosted SVGs from Simple Icons, plus
generated PNG/WebP raster versions used by the UI. Simple Icons is distributed
under CC0-1.0, but brand names and logos may still be covered by trademark rules
and each brand's own guidelines.

The repository intentionally does not bundle app artwork for apps that do not
have a suitable Simple Icons entry. Those apps currently use external vendor or
store artwork URLs in `src/data/apps.json` until we have explicit redistribution
permission or another clearly licensed source:

- Rectangle
- Keka
- TablePlus

Do not add custom lookalike brand icons. Do not commit App Store artwork, website
favicons, or vendor assets unless their license explicitly permits
redistribution in this repository.
